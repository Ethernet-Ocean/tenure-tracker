Who is Oceano Marshall?

I am a proud African American Assistant Professor of Game Design at Long Beach City College. In my first year (Fall 2026), I teach approximately 60 students across game design, UX/UI, and multimedia design.

I am also a video game developer. My original game, High Rollers, a multiplayer arena game for up to four players built in Unreal Engine 5, is published on itch.io and was submitted to four game jams, including the Playtest & Feedback Exchange Jam #10.

I hold a master's degree in Interactive Computer Graphics from California State University, San Bernardino, and a Clear California Career Technical Education Teaching Credential in Arts, Media, and Entertainment.

Representation matters. A student shared with my dean that they had never had an African American digital arts professor before. My goal is to inspire Black students and other underrepresented students to see themselves as future game designers, developers, and creative technology leaders through mentorship, hands-on learning, and industry opportunities.
