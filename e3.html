Q1 · WHAT DID I LEARN ABOUT MY PROFESSIONAL PERFORMANCE?

My students told me that my preparation, individual support and career-focused teaching are working. DMA 15 and DMA 40 respondents were positive on all twelve items; in DMA 15 all seven strongly agreed that I was prepared, gave timely and helpful feedback, and was interested in their success. In DMA 10, all seven respondents were positive on preparation, engagement, responding to questions, explaining the grading system, and interest in their success.

Across all three courses, students named the same strengths: patience, explaining why we learn each skill, hands-on help with technical problems, in-class catch-up time, knowledge of the game industry, and access to internships and opportunities.

DMA 10 also showed me where to grow as a teacher of beginners. Four of seven agreed the subject matter was presented clearly and five of seven that feedback was timely; written comments asked for more visual, step-by-step instructions, more time in each program before switching, and videos tied to each week's lesson. Carolyn Castaño's observation of DMA 10, rated overall Satisfactory, named the same area (clear communication) and recorded that room K143's wall-facing stations and acoustics make it hard for every student to see and hear. My adjustments in Q2 focus on exactly these two things: clearer demonstrations and beginner-friendly pacing.

TWO ONGOING PRIORITIES

1 · CLEARER, MORE VISUAL DEMONSTRATIONS

Post a short agenda each session; show a written step list and a finished example beside every live software demonstration; move to the far side of the room and project my voice so every student can see and hear.

Responds to: DMA 10 item 6 and comments; Castaño's communication criterion; DMA 15 request for “more visual instructions.”

2 · BEGINNER-FRIENDLY PACING

Spend more time in each core program before moving to the next, with clear checkpoints, and link the matching video from my existing tutorial library inside each week's module.

Responds to: DMA 10 comments on pace, switching software and lesson-linked videos; item 9 on timely feedback.

How I will know it worked: Spring 2027 DMA 10 evaluation items 6 (clarity) and 9 (timely feedback), compared with Fall 2026, and my next classroom observation.

Supports already in place that these build on VERIFIED: the Canvas Technology Hub of official software documentation, the Deepen video tutorial library, UDL tutorial options for credit, Kahoot knowledge checks with review of missed questions, and office hours four days a week (E-4 1(g)).
