Proposed Advisory Questions

  1. Do the proposed program and course titles accurately communicate the VR and XR workforce focus?
  2. Are the two courses an appropriate short-term sequence for entry-level preparation and skill upgrading?
  3. Which technical, design, accessibility, collaboration, and portfolio competencies are essential?
  4. Do Unity, Unreal Engine, current VR hardware, real-time 3D workflows, and user testing reflect employer expectations?
  5. What employment outcomes and broader occupational titles should appear in the proposal?
  6. What revisions should be completed before formal curriculum submission?

Proposed Motion

The Game Design and Animation Industry Advisory Committee recommends development of the proposed Virtual and Extended Reality Development Certificate of Completion and its two required noncredit courses, Introduction to Virtual and Extended Reality Development and Virtual and Extended Reality Portfolio Development, subject to incorporation of the committee's documented recommendations and completion of the LBCC curriculum approval process.

Minutes Checklist

  • Motion, mover, seconder, vote result, and any abstentions
  • Separate discussion and motion for the credit Certificate of Achievement if it is also presented
