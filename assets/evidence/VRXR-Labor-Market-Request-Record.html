Full Context Submitted

LBCC is exploring a proposed 9-unit Virtual Reality and Extended Reality Development Certificate of Achievement within Digital Media Arts. The anticipated curriculum includes a new 3-unit introductory VR course, DMA 50: Production Studio, and one existing 3-unit DMA course. Final course selection will be determined through the curriculum and advisory processes.

The certificate would prepare students to develop immersive games, simulations, training applications, and interactive experiences using Unity, Unreal Engine, and VR hardware such as Meta Quest.

Please examine Los Angeles and Orange County demand for entry-level VR/XR developers, Unity and Unreal developers, game and simulation developers, technical artists, 3D artists and animators, and immersive interaction designers. Because VR/XR positions are often listed under broader occupational titles, please also consider job postings containing keywords such as virtual reality, augmented reality, extended reality, immersive technology, spatial computing, Unity, Unreal Engine, and simulation. Data on job postings, projected growth, wages, required skills, employer demand, and relevant industries would be especially helpful.

WHY THIS MATTERS VR/XR job postings frequently use broader titles. The keyword request helps capture immersive-development demand that may not appear under a single SOC code.
