LBCC VR XR Curriculum Stakeholder Summary

Prepared by Oceano J. Marshall | Digital Media Arts | Fall 2026

Project Overview

The proposed curriculum creates connected credit and noncredit pathways in virtual reality and extended reality. Students will develop accessible immersive experiences, real-time 3D projects, and portfolio-ready work using Unity, Meta Quest 3, and Unreal Engine.

Primary Content

  • Unity XR Interaction Toolkit, C# scripting, interaction systems, locomotion, usability, and Meta Quest deployment
  • Unreal Engine environments, Blueprints, lighting, animation, Sequencer, virtual cameras, and virtual production
  • Real-time 3D assets, visual effects, spatial audio, accessibility, testing, optimization, collaboration, and portfolio presentation

Related Discipline Connections

The curriculum connects with Film Production and Television/Emerging Media through cinematography, virtual cameras, Sequencer, lighting, storytelling, editing, and virtual-production workflows. The proposed curriculum is distinguished by its primary emphasis on interactive XR development, headset-based design, real-time interaction systems, and immersive user experience.
