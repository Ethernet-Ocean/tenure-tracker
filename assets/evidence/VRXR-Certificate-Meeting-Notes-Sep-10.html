Purpose and Requested Outcome

Review the proposed nine unit certificate and obtain guidance on the decisions, consultations, documentation, and timeline required to move it through CourseLeaf CIM. By the end of the meeting, we would like to identify the next responsible person and target date for each major step.

Current Proposal

  • Award type: Certificate of Achievement
  • Total units: 9
  • Proposed TOP Code: 0614.10 Multimedia
  • Proposed CIP Code: 10.0304 Animation, Interactive Technology, Video Graphics and Special Effects
  • Exploratory Lightcast request submitted August 20, 2026, for Los Angeles and Orange Counties
  • Center of Excellence labor market request submitted
  • Primary instructional areas: VR and XR development, immersive design, game development, Unity, Unreal Engine 5, prototyping, testing, accessibility, and virtual production

Draft Catalog Description

This program prepares students to design, develop, test, and present immersive virtual and extended reality experiences. Students develop foundational skills in interactive design, real time development, prototyping, user experience, and virtual production while using current industry tools and workflows. Completion supports entry level opportunities in immersive media, interactive entertainment, simulation, visualization, and related digital media fields, as well as continued education in game development, animation, emerging media, and interactive technology.

Guidance requested: Confirm whether this description is appropriate for prospective students and accurately communicates the educational, transfer, and career outcomes without duplicating the program requirements.

Draft Program Learning Outcomes

  1. Design and develop functional immersive experiences using current VR and XR tools and real time development workflows.
  2. Apply interaction design, user experience, accessibility, spatial design, and visual communication principles to immersive projects.
  3. Prototype, test, evaluate, and revise VR and XR experiences based on technical performance and user feedback.
  4. Present a professional immersive media project demonstrating production skills, collaboration, documentation, and career readiness.

Enrollment and Admission

Limitations on enrollment: No program specific limitations are proposed.

Admissions requirements: Open admission with no additional requirements beyond those established by Long Beach City College.

Guidance requested: Confirm whether prerequisites, advisories, recommended preparation, safety requirements, or other restrictions should apply.
