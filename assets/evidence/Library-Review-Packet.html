LBCC VR XR Curriculum Library Review Packet

Prepared by Oceano J. Marshall | Digital Media Arts | Fall 2026

Purpose

This packet supports one library consultation for the related credit and noncredit VR/XR course and certificate proposals. The requested review is to identify appropriate library resources, recommend collection additions, and confirm options for placing selected materials on reserve for first-day student access.
