VR and XR Curriculum Process Meeting Minutes

Long Beach City College Digital Media Arts

Meeting date

September 16, 2026

Meeting format

Curriculum planning meeting

Participants

Oceano Marshall, Colin Williams, Dean Janét Hund, Alison Hoffman-Han, and Dustin Garcia

Purpose

Confirm the curriculum process for the proposed credit VR/XR Certificate of Achievement, noncredit Certificate of Completion, and related course proposals.

Record status

Planning and consultation record. No formal department or advisory vote was taken at this meeting.

The participants confirmed how the credit and noncredit curriculum should be developed, reviewed, and documented. The immediate priority is to complete aligned course and program proposals while maintaining consistent titles, requirements, and supporting evidence.

Confirmed Curriculum Process

  • Courses and programs may be developed concurrently. A program may enter review before all related courses are approved, but it can be held until every required course is approved.
  • Course proposals are reviewed through Course Evaluation. Program proposals are reviewed through the Associate Degree and General Education process.
  • A CourseLeaf proposal must be complete before entering Workflow. Oceano completes editable faculty-author fields; Department Head, Dean, Academic Services, and other assigned reviewers complete their designated fields.
  • The official labor market report belongs with the program proposal rather than the individual course proposals. Drafting may continue while the report is pending.
  • The credit Certificate of Achievement requires a Los Angeles Regional Consortium recommendation. Oceano will notify Colin when the labor market report is received and the program proposal is nearly complete; Colin will submit it to the consortium and enter the recommendation date in CIM.

Parallel Credit and Noncredit Courses

  • Create and finalize the credit course first.
  • Use CourseLeaf's copy function to create the noncredit parallel from the credit course.
  • For a true parallel pair, the title, objectives, student learning outcomes, content, and assignments must remain aligned.
  • Apply the appropriate noncredit, CDCP, hour, grading, and program classifications to the copied proposal.

Timeline and Approval Guidance

Colin advised that new curriculum commonly requires multiple semesters. Approval by the December 2026 Curriculum Committee meeting would be necessary for the fastest possible path toward January 2027 Board consideration, Chancellor's Office processing, catalog development, and possible Fall 2027 implementation. The participants recognized that this schedule depends on complete CourseLeaf records, labor market information, advisory documentation, consortium review, and all required approvals.

Industry Advisory Meeting

Verified date: October 28, 2026. The September 16 meeting record states that the advisory meeting remained scheduled for this date. The meeting time was not recorded in this source and should be confirmed through the official calendar invitation.

  • Invite the full advisory group, Dean Janét Hund, and Alison Hoffman-Han.
  • Charace will coordinate advisory communications and take or coordinate the official advisory minutes.
  • Use the LBCC CTE Advisory Committee Handbook to prepare the agenda, recommendation, motion, vote, and minutes.
  • Before the meeting, finalize the exact program titles and course structures that the advisory committee will consider.
  • The advisory minutes must reproduce the exact titles and requirements appearing in the final CourseLeaf proposals and document the discussion, recommendation, motion, and vote.

Immediate Actions

1. Complete one strong CourseLeaf proposal and use it as the model for the remaining proposals.

2. Finalize the credit course proposals and create aligned noncredit parallels.

3. Finalize exact certificate titles, course requirements, hours, and outcomes before October 28.

4. Prepare advisory materials using the exact titles and requirements planned for submission.

5. Attach the official labor market report to the program proposal and contact Colin for consortium submission when the credit program is nearly complete.

6. Reassess the December 2026 target after labor market and consortium timing are known.

Items Requiring Final Decisions

  • Exact titles of both certificates.
  • Final 18-unit credit course list and required-versus-elective structure.
  • Final work-experience course and unit value, if included.
  • Final number and titles of new courses and their parallel relationships.
  • Final noncredit hours, sequence, CIP code, TOP code, and occupation codes.

Approval Record

No formal motion or vote was recorded during this curriculum-planning meeting. These minutes document consultation and process guidance; they do not replace department approval minutes or the October 28 industry advisory minutes required for the curriculum proposals.
