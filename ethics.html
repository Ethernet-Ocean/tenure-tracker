EVIDENCE VAULT

Having access to student files is not permission to publish them. This showcase follows four rules:

1 · ANONYMOUS BY DEFAULT

Students appear as "Student A", "Student B" and so on. Names, usernames, initials and gamertags that appear in filenames or on screen are left off this site.

2 · NO MEDIA WITHOUT A RELEASE

No student image, video or document is published. Each case study shows a locked evidence panel, not a picture, until the student signs a media release and chooses how to be credited. No generated or illustrative art stands in for student work.

3 · NO RECORDS

Grades, rubric scores and instructor annotations (including the annotated PDF exports) are left out. The site notes only that feedback was given.

4 · HONEST SCOPE

Every file's review depth is recorded below. Courses, assignment goals and software not shown by the evidence are marked Not Verified. Survey percentages always show their respondent counts.

PUBLICATION CHECKLIST

Before any project's media goes live:

  • Confirm the course and assignment for each project marked COURSE UNVERIFIED.
  • Get a signed media release from the student (district/college form). For a student under 18, a parent or guardian signs.
  • Ask how the student wants to be credited: anonymous, initials, or full name.
  • Blur on-screen usernames, gamertags and chat windows. Strip names from filenames and metadata.
  • Confirm that growth sequences (Before → Final) come from a single student before presenting them as one student's growth.
  • Keep instructor annotations, grades and rubric scores off the site.
  • Host approved media in the site repo (not as Drive links, which expose filenames and owners).
  • Record the release date and scope in the private evidence log. Remove media promptly if a student withdraws consent.
